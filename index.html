The infected

Three archetypes ship in the core add-on, each with its own animations and behaviour, in civilian and military dress. Health and damage are yours to tune. Hover a card to see the other outfit.

Slow

Shambler

The slow archetype. Numbers over speed: the wall that keeps coming while you reload.

Fast

Sprinter

The fast archetype. Closes distance in seconds and tackles what it catches.

Crawling

Feral

The crawling archetype. Low, quick and hard to spot in cover and crops.

Built for Game Master

Everything is placeable and configurable from the Game Master interface, on your own scenarios and servers.

  • Place them directlySingle creatures and groups of four, eight or sixteen, plus a mixed horde card, straight from the Outbreak faction in Game Master.
  • Ambient populations and directed wavesLet the sandbox populate itself, or author waves and routes to drive a whole scenario.
  • Zombie Permanent KillsA global setting. In the headshots, explosives and fire mode, ordinary body damage only knocks zombies down; a headshot, an explosion or fire finishes them. Works with ACE Medical hit-zone mods.
  • Placing ignores AI limitOff by default. Turn it on to keep placing creatures by hand after the active-AI budget is full; ambience, directed spawning and reanimation keep their own budgets.
  • Downed is not safeZombies feed on knocked-out survivors, more than one at a time, and finish them unless someone drives them off or revives them.

What changed

Published change notes, straight from the Workshop. Other add-ons are listed by their latest update.

Project Outbreak Zombies 2.4.8 · 2026-10-23

    Project Outbreak Infection v1.4.1 · 2026-10-23

    The S-3 inhaler now faces the right way: the red mouthpiece points at you and goes to your mouth, in first and third person. The spray now takes under 4 seconds (it locked you for over 9), and standing, the arm is held well out from the body. One S-3 capsule now gives five doses. S-3 Suppressant Tablets are back as a single-use option next to the inhaler. The capsule and the tablets are now in the regular faction arsenals. The inhaler has a new texture with an S-3 label and a mouthpiece that looks like a spray outlet, and the capsule has a clearer inventory picture. The inhaler no longer turns invisible in your hand after you give a dose to someone else.

    Credits: the S-3 inhaler and capsule are based on "COVID-19 Oral Vaccine Inhaler Prototype" by Gerardo Justel, and the D-4 syringe on "Syringe Prop" by Medessec, both CC BY 4.0. Changes and links are in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md in the add-on.

    Full history on the Workshop

    Creature Melee AI v1.8.9 · 2026-10-23

    Allied creatures no longer block melee contacts in crowded fights. Adds shared support for creatures that prioritize active enemies over unconscious targets.

    Full history on the Workshop

    Werewolf v0.1.2 · 2026-10-22

    New Werewolf roars, attacks, growls and reactions by Atelier Magicae across all four appearances. Added the selected NaturesTemper wolf howl, preserving its pitch and timing without added reverb. Speech and laughs are excluded.

    Full history on the Workshop

    Project Outbreak Deadwood Lite v0.1.12 · 2026-10-22

    Server owners can now raise the number of infected from the server config: groups kept around each player, groups alive across the valley, groups per top-up and seconds between top-ups. Servers that set none of them play exactly as before. Far groups that walk as Project Outbreak stand-ins now stay counted and turn back into zombies when a player comes near. Needs Project Outbreak 2.4.6 or later. Fixed: after a server restart from a save, the headquarters and their spawn points now come back reliably. With another add-on that also manages seize objectives, the two no longer rebuild each other's tasks over and over, which slowed long-running servers down. Infected-held bases keep their owner and flag after a restart from a save, and an opened mass grave keeps spawning until it is destroyed. The Outbreak flag on the bases' flagpoles now shows the whole banner instead of a stretched, cut-off part of it. A base under attack with no player near no longer keeps spawning more and more infected while its far groups walk as stand-ins. A mass grave now also counts its infected walking as stand-ins far away, so it no longer opens a new wave while the last one is still out there.

    Full history on the Workshop

    Ballista Turret WIP v0.1.2 · 2026-10-01

    Bigger bolts and a heavier sound.

    • Increased ballista bolt damage by 1,000x, from 250 to 250,000. A successful hit can now kill the supported dragon in one shot.
    • Added heavier release, winding and impact sounds for both mounts.
    • AI gunners now hold a usable ballista station during combat instead of repeatedly walking away. Casualties and explicit exit orders still work normally.

    The classic and modern military mounts both use these changes. Still WIP; multiplayer presentation and full-range crew posture need further testing.

    Full history on the Workshop

    Project Outbreak Deadwood v0.1.2 · 2026-10-01

    Taking a district stops its local infected reinforcements; roaming infected can still enter. Graves stop preparing new waves when an attack ends. Nearby infected remain, while abandoned groups clear after everyone leaves. The compact campaign map keeps bases and relays, with field orders in the journal. Joining screens show US Army Remnants. Enemy score icons and flags use the black-and-red Outbreak banner. Infection and Ambience opening overlays default to off. Selected barriers have openings while surrounding fence runs remain. Tunnels have been updated, but zombie movement and spawning underground are still under testing and are not yet reliable.
